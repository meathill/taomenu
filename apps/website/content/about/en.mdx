## What is TaoMenu?

TaoMenu is lightweight QR ordering software for small restaurants, cafés, and takeaway shops. Guests scan a code to open the menu and place orders in the browser. Owners and staff run menus, tickets, and payment notes from a phone.

We do **not** process meal payments for you and we do **not** require a traditional POS terminal.

## Who it is for

- Independent shops that want table QR or counter pickup without a large hardware bill
- Teams that mostly work from phones
- Merchants who need simple menus first, multi-language and AI tools later (Pro)

## How it works

1. Create a store and publish a menu  
2. Print or share table / pickup QR codes  
3. Guests order from the public menu page  
4. Staff advance tickets on the terminal view  
5. Record cash (or other offline) payments and close tables as needed  

## Product principles

- **Phone-first** — day-to-day operations should work on a single device  
- **Clear ownership** — store data is tenant-isolated  
- **Honest scope** — we are ordering software, not a payment processor or marketplace
