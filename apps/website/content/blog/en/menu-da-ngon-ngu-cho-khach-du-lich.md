# Multilingual QR Menus: How to Attract and Serve Foreign Tourists

International tourism is booming worldwide. Major tourist destinations welcome millions of visitors from across the globe every month.

However, for local dining spots, family eateries, and traditional street food stalls, catering to non-native speakers often poses significant challenges:
- Floor staff struggle with conversational foreign languages.
- Foreign guests are unfamiliar with authentic dish names, local herbs, or specialty cuts.
- Dietary preferences and food allergies (peanuts, shellfish, gluten-free, vegan) lead to dangerous misunderstandings.
- Laminated bilingual paper menus quickly become outdated, lack photos, or contain awkward machine translations.

A comprehensive solution to these hurdles is a **Multilingual QR Code Menu**.
