# How to Create a Free QR Menu for Your Restaurant in 5 Minutes

Want to set up a digital QR code menu for your dining establishment so guests can easily browse dishes on their phones, but worried about expensive agency fees or complicated software setups?

With **TaoMenu**, you can build a complete, professional digital menu ready for customer tables in under 5 minutes—all directly from the smartphone in your pocket.

Here is the step-by-step walkthrough.

---

## What You Need Before Starting

All you need is:
- A smartphone or laptop with an internet connection.
- Your dish names, categories, and prices (photos are optional but recommended).

---

## 4 Simple Steps to Create Your QR Menu

### Step 1: Sign Up for Your Store Account
1. Visit [TaoMenu](https://taomenu.app).
2. Enter your email to receive an instant One-Time Password (OTP) or log in with Google.
3. Fill in your restaurant name and business type (e.g., *Corner Bakery*, *Old Town Pho*).

### Step 2: Create Categories and Add Items
1. Open the **Menu Editor** from your dashboard.
2. Tap **Add Category** (e.g., *Main Dishes*, *Beverages*, *Sides*).
3. Under each category, tap **Add Item**:
   - Fill in the item name and price.
   - Add a brief description and upload a dish picture.
   - Set up custom modifier groups if needed (e.g., *Size options*, *Spice level*).
4. Click **Publish Menu** to make your digital catalog live.

![Editing menu categories and items on a mobile screen](/screenshots/nha-hang-pho-xua/menu-editor.webp)

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: If you already have a physical paper menu, simply take photos of each page. TaoMenu's AI Menu Import will scan and extract categories, item names, and prices automatically in seconds!

### Step 3: Generate and Print Table QR Codes
1. Go to the **Table Management** section.
2. Enter the number of tables in your venue (e.g., Table 1 to Table 12).
3. The system automatically creates unique QR codes mapped to each specific table.
4. Click **Download Printable A4 PDF** to print clean, formatted table cards with QR codes.

![Pre-formatted printable A4 table QR sheets](/screenshots/nha-hang-pho-xua/qr-tables.webp)

### Step 4: Display QR Codes on Tables and Start Serving
- Place the printed QR standees or table stickers across your dining room.
- When guests arrive, invite them to point their phone camera at the QR code.
- They will instantly view your full menu with images, descriptions, and current prices.

---

## 3 Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Linking QR codes to large PDF files**: Heavy PDFs take a long time to download on mobile data and require frustrating zooming. Always use lightweight interactive web menus like TaoMenu.
2. **Forgetting to update 86'd items**: Remember to flip items to "Sold out" on your phone before service to avoid disappointing guests.
3. **Printing QR codes too small**: Ensure printed QR codes are at least 2x2 inches (5x5 cm) and avoid placing them under direct glare to ensure quick camera recognition.
