## Summary

This Privacy Policy explains how TaoMenu (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our websites, apps, and related services (the “Service”).

The English version of this Policy is the controlling version.

## Who we are

TaoMenu is operated by Meathill Studio. Contact for privacy questions: [privacy@dyqr.me](mailto:privacy@dyqr.me).

## Information we collect

### Account and store data

- Email address and authentication data (for example OTP or OAuth identifiers)  
- Store profile (name, slug, settings, plan)  
- Menu content, tables, pickup points, orders, service requests, and related operational records you create  

### Device and technical data

- IP address, approximate location derived from IP (for security and locale hints)  
- Browser type, device type, and basic diagnostic logs  
- Push notification subscription endpoints if you enable staff notifications  

### Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar storage that are necessary for login sessions and locale preference. We do not use third-party advertising trackers on the product app.

## How we use information

- Provide, maintain, and secure the Service  
- Authenticate users and protect accounts  
- Send transactional messages (for example login codes)  
- Improve reliability and diagnose errors  
- Comply with law and enforce our Terms  

## Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your region, we may rely on contract performance, legitimate interests (security, product improvement), and consent where required (for example certain notifications).

## Sharing

We share data only as needed to run the Service, for example:

- Infrastructure providers (hosting, email delivery, object storage) under contractual safeguards  
- Professional advisors or authorities when legally required  

We do **not** sell personal information.

## International transfers

Servers and subprocessors may be located outside your country. We take steps appropriate to the nature of the Service to protect data in transfer.

## Retention

We keep account and store data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for backups, disputes, and legal compliance. You may request deletion as described below.

Subject to local law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact [privacy@dyqr.me](mailto:privacy@dyqr.me).
