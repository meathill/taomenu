## What the free plan does

- Takes orders from tables or the counter: guests scan a QR code and orders arrive on the shop's phone.
- 1 staff seat (2 total including the owner) — enough for a small shop or a one-person operation.
- Hand-built menu with 1 language, including photos, prices and options.
- Call-server, payment requests and cash payment recording.
- No time limit: Free is not a trial.

## What the free plan does not do

- No inventory management, financial reports or accounting — this is not a full POS.
- Only 1 menu language; serving foreign guests needs Pro — see [multi-language menus](/menu-da-ngon-ngu).
- No photo/PDF menu import; every item is entered by hand.
- TaoMenu does not collect payments: guests pay the shop directly.

## When to upgrade to Pro

- Need more staff taking orders together (4 staff seats).
- Want a 5-language menu and photo-based menu import.
- Want reviewed AI translation, dish photo enhancement and premium QR templates.

Start free, or compare plans in detail on the [pricing](/pricing) page.
