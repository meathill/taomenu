## Every morning the same: a queue, and a frantic kitchen

Breakfast shops are busy for just two hours a day — but those two hours are chaos: customers crowd to order, staff cook while remembering "pho, less broth, extra onion", the owner counts money and still serves. Breakfast shop ordering software ends that: customers scan a code, pick and customize their meal — orders appear in the staff queue instantly.

## Customers customize: less broth, extra onion, no onion

Every regular has their own preference. Instead of shouting and repeating, customers tap their choices on screen: regular or less broth, extra onion or none. Staff read the order exactly as chosen — no more arguing over a forgotten detail.
