## How it works

1. **Guests scan the QR code** on the table — the code belongs to that table's order flow.
2. **Guests tap "Call server" or "Ask for the bill"** on the menu page.
3. **The request appears instantly on staff phones** with the table number and a message.
4. **Staff handle it and confirm**; guests see the status on screen.

## Who it is for

- Busy shops where guests wave for minutes before staff notice.
- Shops with foreign guests who cannot call staff in words — see [multi-language menus](/menu-da-ngon-ngu).
- Shops that want staff to move less: batch requests instead of walking table to table.

## Things to know

- This is a **free** core feature, not limited to a trial.
- Requests arrive in writing: no shouting, no misheard calls.
- Staff can still walk over when needed; the request is just a signal.

Pair it with [restaurant ordering software](/phan-mem-order-nha-hang) for an end-to-end service flow from ordering to payment.
