## Building a QR menu in a few steps

1. **Build the menu**: enter items by hand (free), or photograph a paper menu and let AI draft it (Pro).
2. **Create the QR codes**: a table code per table, or one shared pickup code for takeaway shops.
3. **Print or share**: print the A4 sheet for tables, or share a link via Zalo, Facebook Messenger.
4. **Guests scan and order** right in the browser — no app install.

## Who it is for

- Shops that reprint paper menus every time prices or items change.
- Shops that want guests to browse photos and descriptions without asking staff.
- Shops serving foreign guests — QR menus display in the guest's chosen language, see [multi-language menus](/menu-da-ngon-ngu).

## Things to know

- A QR menu replaces the **paper menu**; it is not a marketing site or delivery ordering service.
- AI drafts (from photos/PDFs) always need your review before publishing; TaoMenu never publishes automatically.
- Free is limited to 1 language; Pro supports up to 5 languages on the same menu.

## Free or Pro

Hand-built menus are free. Upgrade to Pro for photo-based menu import, premium QR templates and more languages. See the [pricing](/pricing) page.
