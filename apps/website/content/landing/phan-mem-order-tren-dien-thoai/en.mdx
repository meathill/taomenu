## The whole shop runs on one phone

1. **Sign up** from your phone — no computer, no POS.
2. **Build the menu** on your phone: hand-entry is free, photographing the menu needs Pro.
3. **Share QR codes** via Zalo, print them, or stick them on tables.
4. **Take orders on your phone**: staff see orders, mark them done, record payment.
5. **Manage the shop from anywhere**: pause orders, update prices, add items anytime.

Manage the menu right on your phone: add or edit items, update prices, mark sold out — changes go live once you publish.

## Who it fits

- Owners without a computer who live on their phone.
- Small teams where everyone already works with a phone.
- Street and counter pickup shops — see [banh mi shop ordering](/phan-mem-order-quan-banh-mi).

## Minimum requirements

- One smartphone with internet access for the owner.
- Staff use their own phones (no device purchases).
- A printer only if you want to print QR codes; sharing a link works too.
