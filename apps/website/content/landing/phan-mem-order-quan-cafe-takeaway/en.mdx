## Rush hour: the pickup counter is a mess

Mornings and lunch breaks, a takeaway café always has a queue: customers wait to order, staff brew while trying to remember which cup is less sugar, which one extra ice. Cafe takeaway ordering software puts the menu on the phone: customers pick drinks and customize them on screen, orders land straight in the brew queue — staff just follow, no mix-ups.

## Sugar, ice, milk — chosen on screen, no shouting

"Less sugar, extra ice, fresh milk" — instead of shouting across a noisy shop, customers tap each option on screen. Shops also sell more toppings: pearls, fruit jelly, pudding, each with its own price.

## Pickup numbers: customers watch the progress

Every order gets a daily pickup number; customers wait while watching the status on their phone. During rush hour, people who arrive on time grab their cup and go — no more crowding the counter asking "where is my drink?".
