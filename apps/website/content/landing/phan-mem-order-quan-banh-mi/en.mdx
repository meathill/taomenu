## How it works

1. **Place one pickup code** at the counter or on the door.
2. **Guests scan**, pick items and place the order — no seat needed.
3. **Orders get a daily pickup number** (in the shop's timezone, starting from 1).
4. **Staff prepare in order** and call the number when ready.
5. **Guests watch progress on their phones**: preparing / ready.

## Who it is for

- Banh mi, milk tea and fast-food shops busy with takeaway.
- Street shops with few or no seats.
- Shops that want to end the crowd asking "where is my order?".

## Things to know

- Pickup numbers reset per business day in the shop's timezone, not the international calendar.
- No kitchen printer needed: the queue lives on staff phones.
- Guests install nothing: everything runs in the browser.

Combine with a [QR menu for your shop](/menu-qr-cho-quan-an) so guests browse the menu and track their number. See the [pricing](/pricing) page.
