## Creating a multi-language menu

1. **Enable another language** in the Pro menu (up to 5 languages on the same menu).
2. **Translate with AI**: TaoMenu drafts item names, descriptions and options.
3. **Review every entry**: check wording, prices and context; edit as needed.
4. **Confirm and publish**: translations only go live when you approve them.

Guests pick a language on the menu page, or it follows their device language.

Guests pick the language right on the menu page, or it follows their device language automatically.

## Why AI-assisted translation

- Staff do not need to be fluent; AI drafts and you review.
- Familiar dish names stay familiar — you decide what stays unchanged.
- Pro includes 20 translation jobs per month; each item translated counts once.

## Things to know

- AI never publishes translations automatically; everything goes through drafts.
- Translations never overwrite content you wrote yourself for that language.
- Free supports only 1 language — more languages need Pro.
