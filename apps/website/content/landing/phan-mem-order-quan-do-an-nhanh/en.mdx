## Lunchtime: the pickup counter is packed

Fast food shops at rush hour are always crowded: customers line up to order, staff ask back "chicken combo or rice combo?", numbers are shouted over the noise. Fast food ordering software puts the menu on the phone: customers scan, pick a combo and the drink that goes with it — orders land in the queue, staff just follow the numbers.

## Pickup numbers: no more crowding the counter

Every order gets a daily pickup number; customers watch the status on their phone and arrive when it is ready. The shop serves more people at rush hour without adding a single order-taker.
