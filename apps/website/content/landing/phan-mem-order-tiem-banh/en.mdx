## Rush hour: customers crowd the counter

Mornings are the busiest time for a bakery: customers crowd the counter, staff write orders while baking, and buyers anxiously ask "where is my bread?". With bakery ordering software, customers just scan one QR code at the counter and pick items on their phone — no waiting, no crowding.

## Customers pick size, spice level and toppings themselves

Banh mi, pastries and milk tea all come with choices: small or large, mild or spicy, extra toppings. Instead of staff asking and remembering every order, customers choose on their phone — right, every time.

## Customers watch their pickup number on their phone

Every order gets a daily pickup number; customers follow the status on their phone: preparing / ready. Casual customers stop asking, staff stop answering — whoever arrives on time picks up fresh bread right away.
