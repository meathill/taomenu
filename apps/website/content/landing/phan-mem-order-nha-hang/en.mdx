## Getting started in a few steps

1. **Sign up free** with a phone number or Google, right from your phone.
2. **Build your menu**: enter items by hand, or photograph a paper menu and let AI draft it (Pro).
3. **Create a QR code per table** (or one shared pickup code) and print it.
4. **Guests scan**, browse the menu, pick items and send the order.
5. **Staff receive orders on their phones**, cook, mark done and record payment.

## Who it is for

TaoMenu fits sit-down restaurants that still take orders with pen and paper or chat apps — especially when:

- You serve foreign tourists — see [multi-language menus](/menu-da-ngon-ngu).
- Rush hours get busy and staff cannot keep up with table orders.
- You want fewer misheard orders: everything arrives in writing.

## What TaoMenu does not do

- It is not a full POS: no inventory, accounting reports or ingredient management.
- AI drafts never auto-publish — every draft needs the owner's review.
- TaoMenu does not collect payments: guests pay the shop directly. Online payment is in development.

## Free or Pro

- **Free**: 1 staff seat, 1 menu language, manual menu entry. Enough for a small shop day to day.
- **Pro**: 4 staff seats, up to 5 languages, photo/PDF menu import, reviewed AI translation, dish photo enhancement and premium QR templates.
